Record training clips (webcam)

Camera off. Press “Open camera” to frame yourself, or just press Record.
The preview shows exactly what is saved: the centre of the camera picture cut to 4:3 (the KTH shape), up to 640×480, not mirrored, at the frame rate the camera really delivers. Saved to data/recordings/<action>/ with a label file; the classifier does not need to run and no board is needed. Like KTH: camera still (on a table, not in the hand), whole body in the picture, plain background, side view for walking / jogging / running, one action per clip. The subject name keeps one person’s clips together when the data is split into training and test.
What to expect.
  • The network knows six KTH actions and always answers one of them; there is no “nothing” class. It was trained on KTH video (fixed camera, whole person in the frame, plain background) and scores 79.9 % per clip on unseen KTH people. Jogging versus running is its weakest pair.
  • Every frame is prepared like the training data: grey, the whole frame squashed to 128×128, 30 frames/s. The answer of a clip is the class with the largest sum of its finished windows’ scores.
  • Webcam: frames go to the server as JPEG (about 320×240, centre-cropped to 4:3 like KTH), so they are noisier than a decoded video file and the emulator sees some compression flicker as events. A webcam that delivers fewer than 30 frames/s moves further per network step than the training video. Stand back so your whole body is in the picture, in front of a plain wall, and keep the camera still.
  • All tabs share one session: every open tab shows the same stream and the last button pressed wins. The board serves one client at a time; Stop releases it (and so does closing the page, after a few seconds).
